Privacy policy

WhatsApp Archive Sync is a personal service operated by its account owner. It accesses the owner's Google Drive to create, read, update, and delete archive snapshots in the configured archive folder. Its current authorization permits access to all files in that Google Drive account.

Data handled

The linked-device collector stores messages and session credentials on a private Oracle Cloud server. Exported snapshots may contain text, participant identifiers, timestamps, and limited media metadata. The system excludes chats marked archived or with unknown archive state. Attachment content is not downloaded.

Storage, sharing, and retention

The collector database and Google authorization credentials are stored on the cloud server. Filtered snapshots are stored in the owner's Google Drive; the archive folder is shared read-only with the owner's university account so that the connected ChatGPT Drive integration can read it. The scheduled process retains up to three recent snapshots. The server database may contain older retained message history.

Control and deletion

The owner can remove snapshots in Google Drive and revoke the application's access in Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future Drive uploads; server data must be removed separately by the owner.

Data is used only to operate the owner's archive, not for advertising or sale. The service is unofficial and independent of WhatsApp, Meta and Google.
